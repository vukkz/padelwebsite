A flat website design comp, first viewport of a desktop homepage for "Padel House Beograd," a padel court, specialty cafe, and events space in the moat of Belgrade Fortress at Kalemegdan, Serbia. Rendered as a pure flat screen design -- no device mockup, no browser chrome, no laptop, no hands. Serbian Latin copy with correct diacritics (c-caron c, c-acute, s-caron, z-caron, dj-with-stroke). Visual world: Kalemegdan park and public-works wayfinding signage -- vitreous enamel plates, routed timber park signs, cast-iron posts, directional arrow stacks, the kind of municipal signage every fortress visitor walks past. Palette strictly: enamel green #1B5E3A, enamel white #F2F0E9, signal red #B5121B, iron grey #2B2A28. Hard-edged enamel color fields, small chipped edges revealing metal beneath, visible screw fixings at plate corners, subtle specular sheen across the enamel surface, routed timber grain, cast-iron texture. Typography is sturdy condensed grotesk capitals with generous letterspacing, in the manner of municipal signage lettering, with arrow glyphs used as functional wayfinding elements -- NOT a high-contrast serif, NOT warm editorial type. Composition: a large enamel wayfinding plate dominates the frame, mounted over a photograph of the fortress moat showing the padel court below; stacked directional rows on the plate each paired with an arrow glyph reading "PADEL 90' ->", "KAFA ->", "DOGADJAJI ->" (render DJ as dj-with-stroke, single character); the venue name "PADEL HOUSE" set as the plate's header in large signage capitals, with "KALEMEGDAN . BEOGRAD" beneath it in smaller signage caps; a smaller separate enamel plate below rendered as the booking call to action reading "REZERVISI TERMIN" (with s-caron characters); small municipal details throughout -- screw fixings at plate corners, a sliver of cast-iron post at the frame edge, a thin enamel hours strip reading "08-18". Do not invent prices, star ratings, testimonials, or customer names. Finished, art-directed, magazine-quality graphic design craft, flat and legible as a real website hero section.
