## Design Specificity Verdict

**Specific, and unusually so.** A competitor could not paste their content in. The proof isn't the photography — it's the argument structure. `Recenzije` leads with "Ostaju zbog kafe." and shows six reviews that never mention padel. `Cene` says "Ceo teren, ne po igraču." `Događaji` says "Tvrđava kao sala." — a claim only a venue in a fortress moat can make.

**Caveat: specificity collapses at both ends.** The hero renders the club's own tagline half-illegibly, and the closer abandons the thesis — a page that opens "Nije samo padel" closes with "Vidimo se na terenu." over a cropped-torso photo. Primacy and recency are the two least specific moments on the page.

**Deterministic scan:** static CLI detector clean (exit 0) on both `app/page.tsx` and the wider `app components` tree — verified genuine via no-config re-run and a canary test. Nearly meaningless here: the scanner is text-based on .tsx and cannot see computed styles.

In-browser detector found **19 findings across 8 rules**: tight-leading x6, kicker-above-heading x5, image-hover-transform x3, hero-eyebrow-chip, cramped-padding, overused-font, cream-palette, nested-cards.

Convergence: the detector's 5x kicker-above-heading is the mechanical shadow of A's "five consecutive sections share one skeleton". The 3x image-hover-transform corroborates A's catch that TriStvari images have hover-scale but are `<article>`, not links.

## Overall Impression

Craft is high; systems discipline is not. Individual sections are better-made than most agency work; the sequence has no rhythm, and the design tokens' own stated rules are broken repeatedly by the page that defines them.

## What's Working

1. **`Recenzije` turns the evidence's weakness into the argument.** Nearly every real review praises coffee, not padel. Making "Ostaju zbog kafe." the headline — with six verbatim quotes proving it — earns belief instead of asserting it.
2. **The type system has real editorial authority.** Fraunces at 400 with SOFT 30 / WONK 1 reads as a considered room. The latin-ext subset isn't cosmetic — "šancu", "tvrđave", "Košćuška" render without mid-word fallback.
3. **`Cene` as a ruled menu, not pricing cards.** Two rule-t rows, tabular figures, no boxes, no "most popular" badge. Right register for a café-first venue; sidesteps the SaaS pricing-card cliché.

## Priority Issues

### 1. [P1] Best section is the most broken — and `leading-*` is silently dead page-wide
`globals.css:151` sets `.font-display { line-height: 1.02 }` — same specificity (0,1,0) as Tailwind's `leading-*` but later in source order, so every `leading-*` on a display element does nothing. Seven elements combine them; all seven compute to 1.02, including an h2 marked `leading-[1.06]` and all six review blockquotes marked `leading-[1.45]`. Compounding: quotes set in Fraunces at -0.02em tracking (display face doing the longest continuous reading), and no `lang="en"` on the blockquotes under `html lang="sr-Latn-RS"` — WCAG 3.1.2 failure.
**Fix:** move line-height off `.font-display` onto a `.display-tight` class for headlines only, or add `[class*="leading-"].font-display { line-height: revert-layer }`. Set reviews in Barlow. Add `lang="en"`.
**Command:** /impeccable typeset

### 2. [P1] Two identical clay CTAs in the first mobile viewport; three in the last
At 390x844: hero CTA at page-y 488, sticky CTA at y 784 — 296px apart, both visible before any scroll, same label, same rgb(176,73,45), same radius, same arrow. Five accent instances in the first viewport against the token file's "at most once per screen" rule. The comment at page.tsx:77-83 applies exactly this reasoning to the desktop nav button but not to mobile, where 95% of traffic supposedly lives.
**Fix:** gate StickyCta on the hero CTA being off-screen via IntersectionObserver (~10 lines). Demote the Zatvaranje pill to outlined white, or delete the footer clay text link.
**Command:** /impeccable distill

### 3. [P1] The hero renders the club's own tagline illegibly
h1, clay-300 eyebrow, and 15px body all sit on the brightest region of fortress-court.jpg. Mid-band scrim is `via-green-950/40`, far too weak. The "l" and full stop of "padel." dissolve into the court. animate-kenburns drifts the photo under fixed text for 24s, so no single contrast measurement is valid.
**Fix:** scrim to `via-green-950/75`; add a left-column scrim sized to the text column; mobile `object-[38%_70%]`; cap ken-burns at scale(1.06)->(1.10), drop translate; measure at both keyframes.
**Command:** /impeccable audit

### 4. [P2] Five consecutive sections share one skeleton
Manifesto, Recenzije, Događaji, Cene, Lokacija all use narrow left rail (11px eyebrow + Fraunces h2 2.1-2.5rem + paragraph) then wide right rail. Detector independently flagged kicker-above-heading x5. 250-300px of dead space under the left rail in three sections. Every h2 within 0.4rem of every other, so nothing can signal "this one matters more".
**Fix:** three volumes instead of one. Promote Recenzije to full-bleed with h2 at sm:text-[4rem]; demote Lokacija to a compact band at 1.6rem. Inline the eyebrow above the h2 in at least two sections.
**Command:** /impeccable layout

### 5. [P1] Unconfirmed prices rendered as fact at the largest type size on the page
4.000 and 5.000 render at 2.75rem in the brand serif with no qualifier. PRODUCT.md states these are a market estimate, not the club's price list, and principle 5 requires unconfirmed facts to stay visibly unconfirmed. Same problem: unconfirmed street address, and 5 full stars for a 4.9 rating.
**Fix:** one text-[13px] line — "Procena — potvrditi sa klubom." Render 4.5 stars or a single star + numeral. Link "4,9 na Google" to the actual profile.
**Command:** /impeccable clarify

## Questions to Consider

1. If the café is what people actually come for, and the reviews prove it, why is there no action for the café at all?
2. Whose page is this — the club's, or the developer's? Enormous rigor on contrast and crops, almost none on marking which facts are guesses.
3. The reviews are the strongest argument, in the wrong language, wrong typeface, with no source link and no lang. Doesn't "the club's own words outrank ours" oblige designing *for* those words?
