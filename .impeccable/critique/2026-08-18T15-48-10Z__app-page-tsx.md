## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored — but the authorship is editorial and photographic, not structural.**

Strip the photographs and the Serbian and the skeleton is: full-bleed scrimmed hero → three equal image/title/blurb cards → testimonial band on a coloured field → editorial split → closing CTA band. That is the default premium-marketing frame of this period. What makes this Padel House is *content* (the club's own tagline, six untranslated real reviews, four named real events with real partners) and *nerve* (`/kafa` carries no booking CTA at all; the home page's second-largest headline is "Ostaju zbog kafe"). The *form* is borrowed.

Three pieces of evidence:

- **Motion vocabulary is `rise`, `drift`, a 900ms image scale and a 4px arrow.** Not one interaction on this site could only exist for this venue.
- **Type is the safest possible reading of its own brief.** `layout.tsx:22` ships Inter 800 uppercase at `-0.035em` — the most-used premium headline setting on the web right now. The docstring argues it well ("closest obtainable to the Helvetica Now Display they actually use") and lands on the most neutral available result. Separately, PRODUCT.md commits to **Fraunces + Barlow**; the build ships **Inter + Cormorant Garamond**. Both carry `latin-ext`, so the diacritics are safe — the document is what is stale.
- **The locked direction is not in the build.** `.impeccable/surfaces/app-page-tsx.md` specifies Roaster's Panel: kraft `feTurbulence` ground, zero radius, hairline stroked data cells, an authored SVG stamp, overprint misregistration, drenched colour fields. None of it shipped. `stamp-cta.tsx` retired the stamp the brief called non-negotiable, and `globals.css:287-307` still carries `.panel-type`, `.spec`, `.cell` as dead classes. Either the record or the build is stale, and a reviewer reading the record first will judge the wrong thing.

**Failure-mode 1 (generic booking-SaaS/gym):** `/`, `/padel`, `/kafa`, `/dogadjaji` all **PASS** — no pricing tiers, no icon feature grid, no 1-2-3 "how it works", no logo wall. `/padel`'s six-slot timetable as a numbered `<ol>` with 01–06 and 2.4rem tabular times is the best structural idea on the site. `/rezervacija` **FAILS**: below the emerald banner it is an off-the-shelf grid — bordered cells, lucide `Ban`/`Lock`/`Check`, a pill submit. This is not the locked plainer/rounded decision, which governs *register*, not *anonymity*. Nothing on that board says padel, Belgrade, or fortress, and the one venue fact that could carry it at zero cost — the café outlives the last match by an hour — is a 12px legend line.

## What's Working

1. **The reviews are treated as evidence, and there was nerve in leading with them.** `Proof` sets one review at 1.75rem as *the argument* with two beneath as corroboration, under a headline that says the quiet part out loud, and `/kafa` carries all six verbatim in English with `lang` set per quote (`app/kafa/page.tsx:78`). This converts the club's biggest awkwardness — a padel club whose reviews are all about coffee — into the site's thesis, in customers' own untranslated words. It also permanently protects failure-mode 2.

2. **The booking sheet is engineered, not decorated — and B proved it.** All eleven body siblings inert (not aria-hidden), un-inert before focus restore with a comment explaining why splitting them breaks it, focus to the alert on server error, focus to the success heading because the opener has unmounted, slot button pre-focused because iOS Safari will not focus on tap, and the tap-outside backdrop dropped from the a11y tree so the sheet does not announce "Zatvori" twice. Twelve scripted checks, twelve passes, including Tab-wrap in both directions and restore to the exact opener. This was a carried-unfixed P1 two cycles running; it is now finished work.

3. **Unconfirmed facts are disclosed where a club rep will look first.** The `/padel` price block and the `/rezervacija` legend both carry "okvirna procena … ne zvanični cenovnik kluba", on the two screens where 4.000/5.000 is set largest and repeated most. For a spec pitch, being caught inventing a price is terminal; stating the gap at the point of maximum scrutiny converts a liability into evidence of rigour.

## Priority Issues

### 1. [P0] On a phone, the booking page shows nothing bookable in the first screen — then presents availability court-major

Measured at 390×844 for a first-time visitor: header 0–125, emerald banner 126–539, sticky strip ~555, board heading 703, "Teren 1" 793, **first tappable slot y=989** — 145px below the fold. With a stored booking it moves to **y=1214**. "Teren 2" is at 1282, "Teren 3" at 1761.

**Why it matters:** PRODUCT.md requires the booking action to stay reachable without scrolling, and mobile is the declared dominant context. It *is* reachable on `/` (hero CTA y=533, header plate y=18) — and then is not, on the page that CTA leads to. Worse, court-major stacking means the real question — "what is free at 11:00?" — requires three separate scans 490px apart and three remembered answers. The desktop gets a matrix; the dominant context does not.

**Fix:** (a) Collapse the emerald block to one header row below `sm` — keep the date and free count, drop the 2.8rem instruction headline and the 46ch paragraph; that alone lifts the first slot to ≈650. (b) Invert the mobile board to **time-major**: one row per start time, three court chips inside it. This is also exactly what the surface brief's own "VU Meter Bridge → one sweepable ruler of identical units, never separate cards" raise asked for and never got.
**Command:** /impeccable adapt

### 3. [P1] The booking flow switches to the formal *vi* register at the moment of commitment

I grepped this myself: **every** formal-register string outside `/admin` lives in `components/booking/*` — `booking-sheet.tsx:171,175,216,230,332,366`, `cancel-panel.tsx:37,45`, `my-bookings.tsx:79,98`. Everything else on the site is informal: `app/page.tsx:343` "potvrdu **dobijaš**", `app/rezervacija/page.tsx:94` "Potvrdu **dobijaš** odmah", `booking-board.tsx:346` "Termin **te** čeka".

It contradicts itself inside a single file. `booking-sheet.tsx:332` instructs "**Zatvorite i izaberite** drugi termin" — and the button that performs exactly that, 55 lines later at `:387`, is labelled "**Izaberi** drugi termin". The instruction and its own button disagree on how to address the reader.

**Why it matters:** in Serbian this is not tone, it is the declared social relationship. The site spends four pages establishing first-name terms and then, at the exact moment the visitor types their name, switches to the register a bank uses — and switches back three lines later. To a native reader it reads as two people wrote the product. (The two "Zatvaramo ceo prostor za vas" event lines are a defensible corporate-client V-form. Leave those.)

**Fix:** convert every string in `components/booking/*` to T-form: "Unesi ime i prezime.", "Unesi broj telefona…", "Pokušaj ponovo.", "Proveri internet…", "Zatvori i izaberi drugi termin", "Klub te zove samo ako nešto iskrsne sa terminom."
**Command:** /impeccable clarify

### 4. [P1] A 409 destroys the name and phone the user just typed, and offers an instruction instead of a route

On conflict `booking-sheet.tsx:206-213` sets `taken`, disables both inputs, and swaps submit for "Izaberi drugi termin" → `onClose` (`:386`) → `setSelection(null)` unmounts the sheet along with its `useState` name and phone.

**Why it matters:** the collision is the system's event, not the user's. On a phone, re-typing a full name and a phone number *is* the cost of booking. This is the highest-drop-off moment in the product and the one place its DB-enforced-correctness story is on display — the felt experience is "the system took something from me and told me to start over."

**Fix:** lift `name`/`phone` into `BookingBoard` so the next sheet opens pre-filled. Turn the recovery into a route rather than an instruction: offer the same time on another court, or the nearest free time on the same court, as a button inside the alert. That data is already client-side in `courts[].cells`.
**Command:** /impeccable harden

### 5. [P1] The primary control is a blind server round-trip, and there is no error or loading boundary anywhere

`date-strip.tsx:51` pushes to a `force-dynamic` route that awaits Supabase, with no `useTransition`, no pending chip state, and no `app/rezervacija/loading.tsx` — I verified the directory contains only `page.tsx`. Separately, `find app -name "error.tsx" -o -name "global-error.tsx"` returns **nothing**.

**Why it matters:** fourteen chips, each a silent variable-latency round-trip, on the connection profile this project assumes. The user's model is "tap = instant"; the natural recovery for silence is to tap again. And if Supabase is unreachable, the visitor gets Next's default error screen on the one route where "call the club" is a working fallback and `CLUB.phone` is one import away.

**Fix:** wrap the push in `useTransition`, mark the tapped chip `aria-busy` and dim the board while pending. Add `app/rezervacija/loading.tsx` (board skeleton) and `app/rezervacija/error.tsx` carrying `CLUB.phone` and `CLUB.hoursLabel`.
**Command:** /impeccable harden

## Persona Red Flags

**Milica, 34, meeting a friend for coffee at Kalemegdan on a Saturday, iPhone, has never played padel and never will (project-specific).** She is the largest real audience — all 20 Google reviews are hers, not a player's. The frame gives her exactly one action on every page: a terracotta **Rezerviši** plate for a court she does not want. On `/kafa`, the page built expressly for her, that plate is the only filled button on screen — the page correctly refuses her a booking CTA and the frame hands her one anyway. Her actual next question is "where is this and can I park": the answer is one 11px link at `app/kafa/page.tsx:63` pointing at a Google Maps *search*, plus an address in the footer. No map, no "kako doći", no today's hours. And the hero of her page has no coffee in it — `/kafa` opens on `hero-cafe.jpg`, a wooden pavilion and a lawn, while `drinks.jpg`, the only frame with a cup in it, is spent on `/` and again at the *bottom* of her page.

**Low-vision user at 200–400% zoom.** `.label` at 11px/0.2em carries operational content, not eyebrows — "13 slobodnih termina" and "Sreda, 19. avgust 2026." in the `/rezervacija` banner — and it is the exact class carrying all three of the site's AA failures. Separately, `slot-styles.ts:44-48` renders a past slot as `text-muted-foreground/50` on `bg-muted/40` with no label and no price: the visual encoding for "past" is absence.

**Keyboard-only user.** Mostly well served now — the sheet is a genuine trap and the success screen takes focus. Two breaks: `DateStrip` is a `role="group"` of 14 plain buttons in a `no-scrollbar` snap scroller, so chip 9 costs 9 tab stops with no roving tabindex and no arrow keys; and activating "Otkaži" in `MyBookings` replaces the focused button with an inline confirm and drops focus to `BODY`, dumping the user at the top of the document with a full Tab journey back to the confirm they just asked for.

**iOS Safari on cellular — most of this traffic by the project's own assumption.** The sheet is `fixed inset-0 … items-end` with the name input auto-focused on open, and **`visualViewport` appears nowhere in the codebase**. When the keyboard opens, the submit button — at y=703 of an 844 viewport — sits behind it, and `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` does nothing about that. Structurally certain; *unverified* by direct measurement, as no keyboard simulation was available.

## Minor Observations

- **[P2] The cancellation flow has three defects in one screen.** Focus is lost to `BODY` on the inline confirm (above). `--destructive #a92e26` and `--terracotta #b04a2c` are a 7/28/6 RGB delta, so the irreversible "Da, otkaži termin" plate and the "book a court" plate are the same colour at a glance — on the only irreversible control the public site has, on a page that states no undo exists. And the safe choice, "Ne, zadrži termin", is a **113×20** underlined text link beside it, while `MyBookings`' cancel button measures **73×36** — both under the 44px floor the rest of the site holds. Give destructive its own register (outline, red rule, red text), promote "Ne, zadrži termin" to the filled button, and raise both to 44px.
- **The cancellation credential is device-bound and disclosed only after commitment.** The token is returned once and written to `localStorage`; the caveat "Termin te čeka i na ovoj stranici, na ovom telefonu" arrives *after* submit. Book on a friend's phone or in private browsing and self-service cancellation does not exist — the phone rings, which is the exact thing this product exists to stop.
- `app/padel/page.tsx:211` claims "večernji termini rade i kad padne mrak", but `SLOT_START_TIMES` ends at 15:30 (finishing 17:00) and the venue closes at 18:00. `PEAK_START_HOUR = 17` exists for an evening extension that has not happened. Defensible in December, misleading in August — on a page whose neighbouring block carries three honesty disclaimers.
- `/dogadjaji:83` wires `mailto:info@padelhouse.rs` — an address PRODUCT.md lists as **unconfirmed** — to a live action with no disclosure, while the prices two pages over get three. Same in the footer. A club rep clicking it in the room is a bad demo moment. `/dogadjaji` also has no enquiry form: for the highest-value transaction on the site, the only routes are `tel:` and a frequently-inert `mailto:`.
- **`drift` runs 20s `fill: forwards` and never returns.** After it completes the hero photograph sits permanently at `scale(1.10)` — 10% zoomed and cropped, with no explanation for anyone who scrolls back. `prefers-reduced-motion` correctly disables it (`globals.css:386`).
- The home hero passes `crop="object-[50%_48%]"` with **no `lg:` variant**, while all three section pages pass two. Per `page-hero.tsx:44-51` the X value is what acts at phone width, and 50% on this frame lands on sky and rampart wall — not the pavilion, court and terrace the surrounding comment says the frame was chosen for. Desktop gets the whole argument; mobile gets a wall.
- `DateStrip` renders "DANAS" identically to every other chip on a day with zero bookable slots (`?datum=2026-08-18` → "0 slobodnih"), and the page silently opens on tomorrow with no sentence saying why.
- Once the desktop board's header row scrolls away, the court labels are gone and free cells read as anonymous "4.000 RSD" boxes. `aria-label` retains the court, so screen-reader users are better served than sighted ones here.
- The accent rule ("at most once per screen") is unevenly held: `/` 6 uses, `/padel` 3, `/dogadjaji` 1, `/rezervacija` 2, `/otkazivanje` 1 — and `/kafa` **0**, the accent entirely absent from the page whose audience is the site's largest.
- `components/ui/button.tsx:20-26` documents a "clay pill in the hero"; `stamp-cta.tsx` ships a square rectangle. Documentation drift in a codebase that otherwise documents unusually well.
- The `/rezervacija` sticky strip bleeds 4px past the gutter on each side (parent 375, strip 383 at x=-4). No page scroll results — `scrollTo(600,0)` leaves `scrollX` at 0 on every route, and the `scrollWidth: 1102` reading is a Blink quirk from the chip scroller's clipped descendants. Both assessments ran this down before reporting; it is not overflow, but the pairs should match.

## Questions to Consider

1. The site's whole argument is that people come for the coffee — so why is the only persistent action, on all five pages including the one built expressly for that audience, "Rezerviši" a padel court? What does the frame look like if the second action is "Kako doći"?
2. `/rezervacija` is the page the club owner will actually judge, because it is where the notebook gets replaced — and it is the one page with nothing of the venue in it beyond a green banner. What single fact about the physical place could carry the board's *structure* without touching its locked plain, rounded register?
3. The surface brief promised availability as one sweepable ruler of identical units. The build shipped three stacked card lists on a phone. Was the direction abandoned, or only the parts that were hard?
4. Every booking mints one cancellation credential and stores it in one browser. If a fifth of cancellers lose the link, the phone rings anyway — and "the phone stops ringing" is the stated success condition. Does an optional email field cost more than it saves?
5. Inter 800 uppercase at −0.035em is the safest possible reading of "heavy neo-grotesque caps", and the docstring argues it well. If exactly one thing on this site were allowed to be strange, what would you set "NIJE SAMO PADEL" in?
