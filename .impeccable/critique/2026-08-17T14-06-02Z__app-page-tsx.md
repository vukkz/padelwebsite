## Design Specificity Verdict

**The marketing site is specific and good. The booking screen is a different product, built in the previous design system. And neither is the design system that was approved.**

**LLM assessment.** Every locked constraint from the Roaster's Panel comp is reversed in the shipped build — and reversed deliberately, with an argument in a code comment for each: kraft/red/yellow → cream/emerald/terracotta (`app/globals.css:18-52`); 2px outlined cells → 1px, with `components/site-frame.tsx:8-11` explicitly calling the 2px outline "a packaging device [that] reads as loud"; the serif ban → Cormorant Garamond ships on the wordmark and `--font-serif` is exposed globally (`globals.css:146`); the feTurbulence kraft grain → absent entirely; "photography never full-bleed behind text" → the `Closing` section is exactly that (`app/page.tsx:375-391`); the rubber stamp → retired in a comment inside the file still named `stamp-cta.tsx`.

Three of the five carried raises are dead code. **VU Meter Bridge** — three separate `Teren 1 / 2 / 3` sections measured at 390px, not one sweepable ruler. **ANSI Nightboard** — one static `freeCount`, no per-slot scarcity. **Plankton Wake** — `onConflict` calls `router.refresh()` and nothing else (`booking-board.tsx:171-175`); the slot silently greys out. Fossil evidence of the swap remains: `app/rezervacija/page.tsx:74-84` carries two stacked contradictory comments, one describing "the ink field, the spec cells, the square corners" and the next describing "emerald ground, campaign caps." `app/layout.tsx:36-65` still injects a DOM "direction contract" declaring terracotta `#C2593A`, a value `globals.css:38` no longer ships.

**Failure-mode test 1 (generic booking-SaaS/gym): marketing passes, booking fails outright.** The four Persuade pages have no cards, no chips, no shadows, one accent, three colour volumes — they do not read as a template. `/rezervacija` is the archetypal SaaS grid: an 18-cell matrix with `rounded-sm` corners, a 14-chip date strip, a slate-grey legend, a `rounded-full` confirm pill. Swap "Teren 1/2/3" for "Room A/B/C" and it ships for a co-working space unchanged. It is also rendered in the retired vocabulary (`clay-500`, `green-900`, `bone-*`, `eyebrow`, `font-display`) kept alive as aliases at `globals.css:125-147` — the site's most important screen literally runs on the old design system.

## Overall Impression

The craft on the four marketing pages is genuinely high — better than most agency work, and `lib/config.ts` contains real design thinking a template cannot fake. But the project is running three design systems at once: the one that was approved, the one that shipped on marketing, and the one still powering the booking screen. That is not a polish problem, and no amount of `layout` or `typeset` will resolve it. The single biggest opportunity is to decide, in writing, which system is the pitch — then make `/rezervacija` obey it, because that screen is currently both the least specific thing on the site and the only place the product actually happens.

## What's Working

1. **The three-volume colour rhythm and the refusal to decorate.** Cream → emerald → cream, one accent, zero shadows measured on every route. `booking-board.tsx:142-143` even talks itself out of a filled green court header — "green as a background block here was the single loudest thing on the page and it isn't a control." That instinct is exactly right, and it is why the Persuade pages do not read as a template.

2. **`lib/config.ts` is the real design work.** Named real events — "Oaza vol. 1", "Padel & Pour x Bottone x Grace Cocktail", "Žurka bez telefona" — instead of a keyword row that would fit any venue in Belgrade. Verbatim English reviews with per-entry `lang` for WCAG 3.1.2. A visible "Predlog cenovnika — potvrđuje klub" flag on the unconfirmed pricing, which is PRODUCT.md principle 5 honored in code. This is what keeps failure mode 2 from being total.

3. **`/kafa` carries no booking action at all.** No `ActionLink` anywhere in the file — a structural, load-bearing answer to "the reviews prove people come for the coffee." Most builds would have put a "Rezerviši sto" button there and lost the argument. Related mechanical wins: zero empty accessible names across 87 interactive elements on three routes, no heading-level skips on any route, and touch targets down to a single 40x44 offender from ten in the previous run.

## Priority Issues

### 1. [P0] On a phone, there is no booking action anywhere on screen at first paint

Verified at a real 390x844 viewport, not from class strings. The header at 390px contains exactly four visible links: `Padel House`, `Padel`, `Kafa`, `Događaji`. The `Rezerviši` link computes to `display: none` (`site-frame.tsx:101`, `hidden … sm:inline-flex`) and the phone number computes to `display: none` (`site-frame.tsx:93`, `hidden … lg:flex`). The hero's `Rezerviši termin` `ActionLink` sits at document y = 873px on an 844px viewport — `onScreen: false`, 29px below the fold, because the hero stacks image-first on mobile at `min-h-[46svh]` (`app/page.tsx:53`).

**Why it matters:** PRODUCT.md states the booking action must stay reachable without scrolling on a phone, and mobile is the dominant context. This kills the CD-ROM Chrome raise in the only context that counts, and it is the exact failure the comment at `site-frame.tsx:13-17` congratulates itself for having fixed.

**Fix:** show the header `Rezerviši` at all widths — shorten the label at <=640px and move the phone into the mobile nav scroller rather than hiding it. Separately, on mobile put the type panel before the photograph, or cap the image at ~30svh so the CTA clears 844px.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

### 2. [P0] Three design systems are live at once, and the booking screen runs the retired one

Detailed in the verdict above. The measured signature is unambiguous: 0 non-zero radii on the marketing routes, 54 on `/rezervacija` alone; `.font-display`/`clay-*`/`bone-*` aliases still resolving at `globals.css:125-147`; a stale direction contract in `app/layout.tsx:36-65` naming a colour that no longer exists.

**Why it matters:** this is a spec pitch. The artifact under review is not the artifact that was approved, so every downstream activity — this critique, the next iteration, the client meeting — is operating on an object nobody agreed to. And the user walking from `/kafa` into `/rezervacija` crosses a visible seam into what reads as a third-party widget.

**Fix:** make the call explicitly and in writing before another pass. Either re-implement Roaster's Panel against the comp (kraft ground, 2px outlined cells, zero radius, feTurbulence grain, drop Cormorant and `--font-serif`), or formally retire it — rewrite the `CONTRACT` block, delete the dead alias scales, and write the shipped system down as `DESIGN.md` so the detector can actually enforce it. Do not ship a third state.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable document` (capture what actually ships), then `/impeccable polish`

### 3. [P1] 69 AA contrast failures across five routes, concentrated in the design system's own second-tier type role

Measured through a canvas so Tailwind's `oklab()` output resolves correctly — a naive parse gave wrong numbers and was discarded and redone.

| Token | Ratio | Casualties |
|---|---|---|
| `text-ink-faint` `#8a7f6f` on cream | **3.48** | every stat unit — "TERENA", "PO TERMINU"; schedule ordinals; every review byline on `/kafa` |
| `text-cream/55` on `--green` | **2.88** | `/dogadjaji` "Program se menja…" |
| `text-cream/60` on `--green` | **3.18** | "REZERVACIJA" eyebrow; "Predlog cenovnika — potvrđuje klub"; all 6 event meta labels |
| `text-cream/70` on `--green` | **3.77** | "CENE", the "rsd" currency on the largest numerals on the site, and "15 SLOBODNIH TERMINA" |
| `text-cream/75` on `--green` | **4.10** | "RECENZIJE" and all three reviewer attributions in `Proof` |
| `text-cream/40-50` on `--green-dark` | **3.25-4.32** | footer copyright and every footer column head |

Per route: `/padel` 22, `/` 15, `/dogadjaji` 15, `/kafa` 10, `/rezervacija` 7.

**Why it matters:** PRODUCT.md says the project deliberately holds AA with measured ratios documented in code. It does not. The failure is structural, not incidental — `.label` (`globals.css:217-224`) is the entire second-tier type role, defined at 11px and set in low-opacity colour everywhere it appears. The casualties are the units, the currency, the scarcity count, and — worst — the pricing disclaimer that principle 5 exists to protect.

**Fix:** on `--green` nothing below 85% cream clears AA; on `--green-dark` nothing below ~58% does. Set those floors as tokens and enforce them. Darken `--ink-faint` to ~`#6b6153`. Where a label must recede, use size and letterspacing, not opacity.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`

### 4. [P1] The booking dialog is `aria-modal="true"` but does not trap focus

Verified by interaction, not inference: four `Tab` presses from the auto-focused name field moved focus out of the sheet onto a link in the footer behind the overlay, with the focus ring visible. `booking-sheet.tsx:45-56` handles initial focus and Escape but installs no trap, never marks the background `inert`/`aria-hidden`, and does not restore focus to the triggering slot on close. Compounding: the auto-focused name field draws a terracotta `--ring` in the same red-orange family as `--destructive`, so the first thing the highest-stakes form does is look like it is already wrong.

**Why it matters:** a keyboard or screen-reader user is told the background is hidden and is then dropped into it, mid-transaction, at the site's only irreversible action.

**Fix:** trap Tab/Shift+Tab within `panelRef` (the ref already exists and is unused), set `inert` on the page container while open, `focus()` the originating button in cleanup, and differentiate the focus ring from the destructive hue.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### 5. [P2] The sticky date strip parks 17px underneath the header — at both breakpoints

Measured at 390px: header height 81px, `z-index: 40`; strip computed `top: 64px`, `z-index: 20` (`app/rezervacija/page.tsx:119`, `sticky top-16 sm:top-20`). Assessment A measured the desktop case independently: header 97px, strip `top: 80px`. Same 17px overlap at both sizes — the offsets were written against the header's content height, not its box. The chips' top edges and the "DANAS / UTO / SRE" weekday caps are clipped on every scroll.

**Why it matters:** the day picker is one of only two controls on the Operate surface, and its labels are cut off exactly when it is doing its sticky job.

**Fix:** set `--header-h` once and have both the header's height and the strip's `top` consume it. Stop maintaining two numbers that must agree.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (distracted, one-handed, mobile) — worst hit, and she is the primary user.** At 390px: no `Rezerviši` in the header, no phone number, and the hero CTA 29px below the fold — measured, not estimated. Reaching the board then needs a horizontal swipe through 14 chips inside a vertically-scrolling 7,009px page whose scrollbar is suppressed by `.no-scrollbar`, with only a 48px gradient fade (`date-strip.tsx:33-37`) hinting days 3-14 exist. When she scrolls, the strip's weekday labels are clipped. Then the board hands her three separate court sections she must scroll past to compare 08:00 across courts.

**Sam (screen reader + keyboard).** Focus escapes the `aria-modal` dialog into the footer — verified. Focus is never returned to the slot button after close. The board is a CSS grid of 36 `<button>`s, not a `<table>`: the `aria-label`s are well written ("Teren 1, 08:00, Slobodno, 4000 dinara") but there is no row/column structure, so there is no way to traverse "all of Teren 2" or "everything at 12:30." And every unit label he needs — "TERENA", "rsd", "15 SLOBODNIH TERMINA" — sits between 3.4:1 and 4.1:1.

**Jordan (confused first-timer).** The grid shows "Stalni termin — firma Delta" and the legend defines it as "Stalni termin" — circular, and he cannot tell whether it might free up. Nothing on `/rezervacija` says rackets are rentable; that lives only on `/padel`. And the one line that matters most to him — these prices may be wrong — is the lowest-contrast text on the page at 3.18:1.

**Milica, the café-first Saturday visitor (derived from PRODUCT.md).** She arrives from Google Maps wanting to know if she can just come for a coffee. `/kafa` answers in prose and gives her nothing to do: no map, no menu, no hours block, no prices. The only button in the persistent frame says REZERVIŠI — the one action she explicitly does not want. Her page's h1 is "NIKO NE MORA DA IGRA": a sentence about padel. And the one link that would help her is mislabelled — `app/kafa/page.tsx:64-71` renders "4,9 · 20 recenzija · Google →" over an `href` that is a Google Maps search URL. The site's own thesis is that Milica is the majority customer, and she is the only persona with no successful path.

## Minor Observations

- The Trio images are ungraded while every `PageHero` image is graded. `page-hero.tsx:56` applies `brightness-[0.92] contrast-[1.14] saturate-[0.75] sepia-[0.3]` plus a multiply wash; `app/page.tsx:199-205` applies none. The Kafa card is a raw multicolour plaza with a pink neon "BGSM" sign fighting the palette — the exact problem the hero comments describe at length and then solve only for heroes.
- `globals.css:146` exposes `--font-serif: var(--font-wordmark)`, so any `font-serif` utility silently pulls Cormorant. A loaded gun under a no-serif rule.
- "4.000" renders 15 times on one weekday board (verified). If price never varies within a day, price should not be the boldest element in every cell.
- `sentenceCase(dateLabel)` produces "Utorak, 18. avgust 2026." in `.font-display` — the only sentence-case heading on the site, on the most important screen.
- `Legend` (`booking-board.tsx:240-256`) is the only place using slate-grey `text-xs` body copy; it reads as a component from a different library, because it effectively is.
- The success screen delivers no artifact — no `.ics`, no reference number, no email/SMS promise. "Klub je obavešten o rezervaciji" is an unverifiable assertion attached to a pay-on-site booking with a 4-hour cancellation window. The user's only rational move is to screenshot the page.
- `.drift` (`globals.css:317-328`) runs a 20s `forwards` scale on every hero image on every route, with no `will-change` and no `@media (hover)` gate.
- Home's `Proof` shows 3 of 6 reviews; "Sve recenzije →" leads to `/kafa`, which shows the same 6 — so "all reviews" is 6, half of which the user just read.
- Detector-side caveats worth recording: `puppeteer` is not installed, so CLI URL scanning is unavailable and all runtime evidence came from injected JS; `resize_window` is broken in this harness (destroyed a tab once, then silently no-op'd), which is why every mobile figure came from a same-origin 390px iframe.
