## Design Specificity Verdict

**Specific in words, generic in image — the hero shipped last session is the reason.**

The copy is the strongest asset and cannot be lifted onto another venue. The type system is engineered rather than styled (`line-height: 1` for Serbian carons; `@layer components` so `.font-display` stops killing `leading-*`).

But all four heroes at 390px are indistinguishable dark-green rectangles. Differentiation is 100% headline text; the bottom ~150px of every hero is flat `green-dark/95` with zero image information. On mobile the venue — the entire pitch — is invisible.

The site contains its own disproof: `Closing` (`app/page.tsx:316`) uses the same `fortress-court.jpg` scrimmed in `--shade` with no filter chain, and it is the best-looking block on the page. The identical photograph appears twice on one page, once dead, once alive.

## Overall Impression

The marketing pages are better-written than most agency work and the booking flow's error handling is genuinely well built. But two of the three changes shipped last session made things worse, and the codebase had already written down why one of them would. The biggest opportunity is to undo the green scrim — green was not the wrong palette instinct, it was applied to the one layer that cannot take it.

## What's Working

1. **The copy is the design.** "Ceo teren, ne po igraču" pre-empts padel's commonest pricing confusion in a headline. Naming actual events instead of listing categories is the difference between claiming capability and showing it.
2. **The booking sheet's restraint.** Two fields, `enterKeyHint`, `inputMode="tel"`, safe-area padding, and a 409 race handled with a distinct visual state and an escape route. Most booking flows ask for email, notes, player count and a marketing checkbox.
3. **Mechanically clean where it counts.** Zero box-shadows on every route, zero heading-level skips on all five, zero interactive elements with an empty accessible name across 74 interactives, and no real horizontal overflow (the 1102px `scrollWidth` on `/rezervacija` is a Chrome propagation quirk — `scrollTo(600,0)` leaves `scrollX` at 0; both assessments ran it down before reporting).

## Priority Issues

### 1. [P0] The green hero scrim destroys the photography — and globals.css predicted it

`components/page-hero.tsx:120`. The filter chain plus a `#5c3a1c` multiply wash plus a `green-dark` vignette plus three `green-dark` gradients, the bottom one terminating at `/95`, reduces four distinct photographs to one green field. `globals.css:44-52` states verbatim: "Deliberately NOT the brand green. Layering a cold #02301a over golden-hour photography turned the terracotta court brown-grey and the warm wood olive — it drained the exact warmth that makes this brand read as expensive." `#02301a` is the exact value used.

**Why it matters:** the fortress is the one asset a competitor cannot copy, and on mobile it is spent for nothing. The hero-to-cream seam is a flat-dark-to-flat-cream butt joint — the "two sites bolted together at the fold" complaint that triggered the redesign is re-created, not solved.

**Fix:** revert PageHero's scrims to `--shade`, drop `sepia`/`saturate`, cap the bottom gradient at ~`/80` so the last band still contains photograph. Keep the cream dateline. Bridge the seam with a `cream-warm` top band on the following section. Also `fortress-court.jpg` opens and closes the home page — pick one.
**Command:** /impeccable audit

### 2. [P0] The mobile nav was broken while fixing the mobile CTA

`components/site-frame.tsx:61-89`. Measured counterfactual at 390px: nav has 87px usable against 214px of content with the always-visible CTA; 212px with it hidden as before. The change hid 127px of nav. "Kafa" is clipped mid-word, "Događaji" is entirely invisible, with no fade, chevron or scrollbar. `/padel`, `/kafa`, `/dogadjaji` and `/rezervacija` have no Trio and no footer nav, so those pages have no discoverable route to the other sections.

**Fix:** below `sm`, drop the nav out of the header row and place the three links as a full-width row directly beneath it. Not a hamburger — the file's own comment is right that hiding nav behind a breakpoint was the original site's worst failure, and a clipped scroller is that failure wearing a different hat.
**Command:** /impeccable adapt

## Persona Red Flags

**Milica, 34, Saturday 09:40, one hand, pushing a stroller (project-specific).** She is the primary user and the site's thesis is built for her. On a phone in bright sun she sees a dark green rectangle, a headline saying "not just padel", and a header offering exactly one word: PADEL. The thing that would decide her — what the place looks like, is there shade, can she get a stroller down there — is behind the scrim. `/kafa` answers all of it ("Mirno, čisto, sa decom i psima… parking je u blizini") and she cannot find it: it is the clipped word behind the Rezerviši button. **Jordan (first-timer).** Three unexplained concepts at once on `/rezervacija`: three courts with no stated difference, "Stalni termin — firma Delta" as a slot state, and a price with no indication it covers four players — that fact lives on `/padel`, which he skipped. ## Minor Observations

- The accent rule is broken page-wide: 9 terracotta uses on `/` (6 distinct sites; the three Trio "Pogledaj →" links are the main offenders), 3 on `/padel`, 6 on `/rezervacija`. Only `/dogadjaji` satisfies one-per-screen. The earlier "one per hero" verification was true but too narrowly scoped to imply the page rule held.
- `.display` sets `font-variation-settings: "opsz" 32`, a no-op unless `--font-inter` resolves to a variable face with an `opsz` axis.
- `.drift` runs `forwards` over 20s then stops permanently; scroll back after 20s and the hero is a static 10%-cropped photo with no explanation.
- `/padel`'s hero pairs a one-word h1 ("Padel") with a one-word dateline ("Teren") — two generic words stacked, the weakest of the four.
- `/dogadjaji`'s hero is a close, recognisable shot of a bride and groom. On an uncommissioned spec pitch that is a consent question worth raising before presenting.
- One touch target under 44x44 on every route: the "Kafa" nav link at 40x44 — width only, because the word is short.
- The success screen greets via `customerName.split(" ")[0]`, so a single-word entry passes validation and greets by surname or nickname.
